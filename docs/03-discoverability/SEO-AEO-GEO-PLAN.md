# Search, Answer Engine and Generative Engine Discoverability Plan

**Version:** 0.1 | **Date:** 24 November 2026 | **Status:** Proposed strategy

## 1. Objective

Make Teedee's approved company and service information easy to discover, understand, crawl, interpret and cite across conventional search engines, answer engines and generative search experiences.

SEO, AEO and GEO are practices, not guarantees. The project cannot promise a particular Google ranking, featured snippet, AI citation or lead volume.

## 2. Search-engine optimisation (SEO)

### Technical SEO
- Ensure indexable pages return meaningful content and correct HTTP status.
- Unique title and description per page.
- Canonical URLs using the approved primary domain.
- XML sitemap containing only canonical, indexable public routes.
- robots.txt consistent with the sitemap and environment.
- Noindex for CMS, preview, draft, staging and other private routes.
- Clean, descriptive URLs and controlled redirects.
- Correct Open Graph and social metadata.
- Fast, mobile-usable pages and stable layout.
- Semantic headings and meaningful internal links.
- Image dimensions, compression and descriptive alt text.
- Structured data that matches visible and verified content.

### On-page SEO
- One clear page purpose and primary topic.
- Clear H1 and logical subheadings.
- Helpful, specific content rather than keyword repetition.
- Service pages that explain scope, use cases, planning considerations and next steps.
- Natural language reflecting how customers describe their problems.
- Internal links between related services and supporting resources.
- No duplicate pages created solely to target near-identical keywords.

### Local SEO
Only use location-specific content and LocalBusiness schema once Teedee confirms its business address, service area, phone, hours and eligibility. Do not infer an office address or create fake local office pages.

Potential setup tasks after approval:
- Google Search Console verification.
- Bing Webmaster Tools verification.
- Google Business Profile audit/claim, if eligible and company-authorised.
- Consistent company name, address and phone where publicly displayed.
- Review of local service intent and actual service coverage.

## 3. Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)

Structure useful answers so search and answer systems can identify the question, response and supporting context.

- Use clear page titles and descriptive section headings.
- Answer common questions directly before expanding with context.
- Create technically reviewed FAQs based on real customer questions.
- Explain acronyms and technical terms in plain language.
- Provide accurate service boundaries and relevant caveats.
- Keep business identity and contact information consistent.
- Use structured data only where content qualifies and is visible.
- Avoid schema markup that claims reviews, ratings, prices, hours or credentials not actually present.
- Provide concise, useful process and preparation guidance.

## 4. Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

Improve the likelihood that systems can interpret and reference Teedee's information without attempting to manipulate answers.

- Publish original, specific and fact-checked company information.
- Make service descriptions, locations and business identity unambiguous.
- Include useful explanations, definitions, decision criteria and practical questions.
- Show real evidence where permission allows.
- Attribute authorship/review responsibility for technical educational material where appropriate.
- Maintain consistent naming across website, company profiles and approved external references.
- Keep important content crawlable and not hidden behind client-only rendering.
- Use stable canonical pages and clear source context.
- Avoid fabricated authority signals, mass-generated low-value pages and keyword stuffing.

Do not add an llms.txt file or special markup merely as a ranking trick; evaluate current standards and actual utility before adoption.

## 5. Structured data candidates

Potential types, subject to factual and eligibility review:
- Organization or LocalBusiness.
- WebSite and WebPage.
- Service.
- BreadcrumbList.
- Article / BlogPosting for approved articles.
- FAQPage only where current search-engine rules and visible page content justify it.

Structured data must reflect visible page content and verified facts. It does not guarantee rich results or AI visibility.

## 6. Content plan

Initial content clusters to validate:
- Company and integrated systems capability.
- CCTV and electronic security.
- Access control, smart locks and gate automation.
- Solar/inverter and power systems.
- Electrical installations.
- Networking/connectivity.
- Building automation.
- Project planning, site assessment and installation process.
- Customer questions and maintenance considerations.

The company must approve the actual services and exact technical scope. Do not assume every category in archived materials is currently offered.

## 7. Measurement and tools

After Teedee approval:
- Configure Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Consider privacy-reviewed analytics.
- Define conversion events for approved enquiry actions.
- Record baseline index coverage, impressions, clicks, query themes and technical health.
- Review search performance at agreed intervals after launch.

Do not install tracking or cookies without the required company/privacy approval.

## 8. Release checklist

- [ ] Primary domain and canonical host confirmed.
- [ ] Page titles and meta descriptions reviewed.
- [ ] Sitemap generated and validated.
- [ ] robots.txt validated.
- [ ] Preview/admin routes excluded from indexing.
- [ ] Open Graph metadata and images checked.
- [ ] Structured data validated against visible facts.
- [ ] Internal links and redirects tested.
- [ ] Search console ownership controlled by Teedee.
- [ ] Analytics and consent decision documented.
- [ ] Mobile usability and page performance measured.
- [ ] No invented business facts or location claims.
