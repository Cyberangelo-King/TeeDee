# Assumptions, Dependencies and Constraints Register

**Version:** 0.1 | **Date:** 24 November 2026 | **Status:** Living register

## 1. Confirmed project facts

- The current engagement covers the corporate website and custom Website CMS.
- The agreed headline project fee is ₦120,000.
- A 50% initial payment (₦60,000) was stated as due by 24 November 2026.
- The target is to prepare the site for rollout and sharing from January 2027, with work planned through the end of 2026.
- Teedee should own production infrastructure and the developer should work through named, authorised access.
- The existing GitHub repository is Cyberangelo-King/TeeDee and its current default branch is main.
- The repository already contains a Next.js website and Netlify static-export configuration.
- Existing archived project specifications and a strategic product proposal are available for reference.

## 3. Dependencies

- Company email and recovery custodian established.
- Company GitHub organisation or clear plan for transferring the repository to it.
- Domain selection, purchase approval and registrar access.
- Netlify team/site ownership and developer invitation.
- Database/auth/storage provider approval, account ownership and billing decision.
- Verified company profile, services, audience priorities, contacts and address/service-area wording.
- Approved logo, brand files, installation imagery and video.
- Written permission to publish identifiable customer sites, client logos, testimonials or project evidence.
- Approved privacy notice and form data-handling process.
- CEO/technical reviewer availability for decisions and testing.

## 4. Constraints

- Existing Next.js project currently builds as a static export. Authenticated CMS and live content publishing require an architecture change or a carefully defined integration strategy.
- The delivery window is short relative to the combination of website, custom CMS, domain/infrastructure, content and QA work.
- The current fee requires strict scope control and the avoidance of unapproved product expansion.
- Third-party services may impose recurring charges, usage limits or regional constraints.
- Content cannot be made accurate by design or code; company verification is required.
- Media quality and permissions may constrain the final visual treatment.
- Production launch must not precede security, content and account-ownership checks.

## 5. External costs to budget separately

Potential costs include domain registration/renewal, business email, hosting upgrades, database plan, storage/egress, transactional email, analytics products, stock-media licences, video production/editing and future maintenance. The service, price, renewal period and payer must be recorded before purchase.

## 6. Treatment of uncertainty

Where a dependency is unresolved, the affected work should be marked Blocked or Provisional. Do not work around missing approvals by inventing facts, using personal accounts as permanent owners or silently substituting paid services.
