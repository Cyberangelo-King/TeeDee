## 2. Purpose and business case

The project will give Teedee a credible, informative and commercially useful digital presence, supported by a company-managed publishing system. The website should help potential customers understand the company's verified capabilities, assess whether Teedee may be relevant to their needs, and make a clear enquiry.

The CMS should reduce the need for developer intervention for routine, approved content changes. It should provide authorised company users with a usable and governed way to maintain public information without exposing technical infrastructure or giving every editor unrestricted system access.

## 3. Objectives

1. Present Teedee's approved identity, capabilities and value in a clear, distinctive and trustworthy way.
2. Improve organic discoverability through sound SEO, answer-engine readiness and generative-engine discoverability practices.
3. Make the site responsive, accessible, performant and useful on mobile and desktop.
4. Provide a working and secure custom CMS for the agreed website content types.
5. Establish a functioning enquiry journey with clear ownership and privacy controls.
6. Place the domain, source code, hosting, database and related infrastructure under Teedee's ownership, with the developer operating through named, authorised access.
7. Leave the project maintainable, testable and documented for future engineering work.

## 4. Scope statement

The current engagement covers the corporate website and its custom Website CMS, including discovery, requirements, design direction, engineering, content integration, infrastructure configuration, testing, launch preparation and agreed onboarding/knowledge transfer.

The broader Teedee digital ecosystem may be considered in architecture and strategic planning, but future products are not to be implemented as part of this engagement.

## 5. High-level deliverables

- Approved project brief, PRD, TRD, scope baseline and roadmap.
- Reviewed existing-repository baseline and documented technical decisions.
- Responsive corporate website using verified company content and approved media.
- Custom Website CMS for the content types and workflows approved in the PRD.
- Domain, DNS, hosting and database configuration as approved and funded by Teedee.
- Search and social metadata, technical SEO foundations, structured data where justified, and AEO/GEO content foundations.
- Working contact/enquiry flow, tested against an approved destination.
- Quality assurance evidence and launch-readiness sign-off.
- Initial CMS administrator/editor orientation and final handover package, subject to the agreed training format.

## 6. Success criteria

The project is successful when:

- Teedee approves the public copy, identity usage, services and contact details.
- Agreed website routes render correctly on supported mobile and desktop sizes.
- The enquiry journey reaches the agreed company destination and produces understandable success/failure feedback.
- Authorised CMS users can perform the agreed create, edit, review, publish and media tasks.
- Unauthorised users cannot access protected CMS actions or unpublished content.
- Public pages expose meaningful content to search crawlers and have page-specific metadata.
- The site meets the agreed accessibility, performance, security and functional acceptance checks.
- The primary domain and production infrastructure are controlled by Teedee.
- Teedee receives the final, verified documentation and can operate routine publishing without relying on the developer for every content edit.

Performance and search improvements must be measured; no specific search position, lead volume, conversion rate or Core Web Vitals score is guaranteed.

## 7. Governance and authority

Teedee retains authority over company facts, public claims, brand approval, legal content, account ownership, spending and launch approval. The developer has authority to make day-to-day implementation choices within the approved requirements and to recommend changes where evidence supports them.

No infrastructure purchase, paid subscription, domain registration, scope expansion or public launch should proceed without the required company approval.

## 8. Commercial and scope control

The current headline fee is ₦120,000, with a 50% initial payment stated as due on 24 November 2026. The remaining payment milestones and any other commercial terms must be governed by the written agreement accepted by both parties.

External costs such as domain registration, mailbox subscriptions, paid hosting tiers, database plans, paid media, stock licences, email delivery providers or analytics products must be explicitly identified and approved. They are not assumed to be included in the developer fee.

Any request that materially changes deliverables, complexity, schedule, third-party costs or support obligations must be recorded as a change request and accepted before the extra work begins.

## 9. Principal constraints

- The existing repository is already a static-export Next.js website; a CMS will require a deliberate architecture and deployment decision.
- Company facts, service boundaries, media permissions, privacy wording and domain details require validation.
- Delivery timing depends on timely content, access, decisions and approvals from Teedee.
- The fee and delivery window require disciplined prioritisation. Advanced CMS capabilities or new products must not enter by implication.
- Production services must be owned and recoverable by Teedee.
