## 2. Company identity

### Document-supported facts

- **Name displayed on both covers:** TEEDEE EDGE AUTOMATIONS LIMITED.
- **Expanded legal-style form used in body copy:** Teedee Edge Automations Limited.
- **Business category described by the company:** professional security and safety solutions, systems design, installation, automation and related training.

### Working public-name recommendation

Use **Teedee Edge Automations Limited** in formal footer/legal contexts and **Teedee** as the conversational brand name, subject to owner approval. Preserve the spelling **Teedee** (not TeeDee) throughout the current project.

### Company proposition in the supplied material

The newer profile uses the line:

> “we don't just install security systems, we give you peace of mind.”

It also says the company takes time to understand each customer's security needs before recommending or installing a solution, with an aim to deliver effective, practical and tailored solutions.

**Interpretation for website positioning (proposed, not approved):** Teedee should be presented as a security, safety and integrated systems company that assesses a property's or organisation's needs, designs an appropriate solution, installs it, tests it and can provide ongoing service/maintenance where agreed.

## 3. Company overview — extracted content

The profiles describe Teedee as specialising in mapping out/designing and installing professional security and safety solutions for personal/residential buildings and corporate organisations of different types and sizes in Nigeria.

The newer profile adds the following differentiators:
- begins by understanding the client's unique security needs;
- recommends or installs only after that understanding;
- aims for effective, practical and tailored solutions;
- positions Teedee as a trusted partner for homeowners and business owners.

**Editorial note:** The exact phrase “trusted go-to partner” is marketing copy, not an independently measured customer outcome. It may be used only with owner approval.

### CCTV delivery methodology

The newer profile explicitly lists:
1. Site survey and assessment.
2. Custom system design.
3. Camera selection and placement.
4. Video footage and storage planning.
5. Power supply assessment.
6. Professional installation and testing.
7. Ongoing service and maintenance.
8. Product and service warranty.

The older profile lists substantially the same sequence, using “video footage audit” and “power requirements” language.

**Website implication:** A process-led CCTV page can be built around assess → design → select → plan storage/power → install and test → maintain. Confirm the exact scope and meaning of “warranty” before publishing warranty terms.

### Access-control details

The profiles support the following descriptions:
- RFID card-based access.
- Biometric access options; the newer profile names fingerprint and facial recognition.
- Centralised management across business premises, including remote locations.
- Standalone access-control solutions for smaller offices and homes.
- Hotel door-locking systems with audit trails (2024 profile; confirm current offering).

**Do not infer:** supported device brands/models, cloud features, integrations, anti-passback, visitor management, attendance/payroll integrations, or specific biometric accuracy.

### Alarm and fire-safety details

The profiles mention intruder/burglar alarms and standalone, conventional and addressable fire alarm systems. The 2024 profile cautions that burglar alarms should be considered one element of a complete security package.

**Website implication:** Explain alarms as one layer in a wider safety/security design rather than promising that an alarm alone prevents burglary or fire loss.

### Electric-fence model

Both profiles describe three layers:
1. Deterrence through the visible presence of the fence.
2. A non-lethal electric response when a breach is attempted.
3. Activation of an embedded intruder alarm to alert nearby people/neighbours.

**Publication caution:** Avoid sensational language (the 2024 profile says “nasty” shock). Confirm applicable installation, warning, safety and compliance practices before publishing technical or legal claims. Do not describe the system as guaranteeing prevention of intrusion.

### Networking and intercoms

The profiles describe structured LAN and WAN networks, organisational efficiency, high-quality equipment and intercom installations from simpler residential systems to enterprise-scale infrastructure.

**Do not infer:** specific network certifications, managed IT support, cybersecurity services, fibre-optic capabilities, bandwidth guarantees or 24/7 network operations.

### Solar and inverter systems

The profiles position independent solar-inverter power as a way to support security-system continuity where grid supply fluctuates or generator operation may be interrupted. The newer profile says Teedee designs and installs systems to keep security infrastructure running around the clock.

**Publication caution:** Treat “around the clock” as an intended design outcome, not a universal guarantee. Actual runtime depends on system sizing, load, battery condition, sunlight, installation and maintenance. Do not promise uptime without a site-specific design and approved specification.

### Smart homes and building automation

This service appears in both service lists, but neither profile provides a detailed list of devices, platforms, integrations or automation scenarios.

**Action:** retain as a service category in the internal catalogue; obtain owner/technical detail before writing a detailed public service page.

- Biometrics solutions and access control.
- Electric fencing.
- Networking and related topics.

The newer profile explicitly mentions training for individuals and corporate teams; the older profile says courses are generally focused on corporations and their upskilling/refresher needs.

**Unconfirmed:** course names, duration, fees, prerequisites, certification/accreditation, training venue, delivery format, trainer credentials, cohort size and upcoming dates. Do not describe the training as accredited or certificate-awarding unless verified.
