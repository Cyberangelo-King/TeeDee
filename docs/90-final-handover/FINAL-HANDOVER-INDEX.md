## Training and knowledge-transfer standard

Training should be practical, role-specific and demonstrated against the actual production or approved training environment.

The planned CMS session should cover:
- safe sign-in and account recovery;
- dashboard navigation;
- creating and editing content;
- previewing content;
- image upload, crop guidance and alt text;
- SEO title/description fields;
- review and publication permissions;
- correcting a mistake and restoring content where implemented;
- enquiry handling if an inbox is part of the approved CMS;
- what staff must never change or share;
- how to request technical support.

The final training pack should include a quick-start guide, a full user guide, example exercises, common troubleshooting steps and a record of who attended. Training duration, participants and whether a recording is needed must be agreed with Teedee.

## Handover acceptance

Handover is not complete merely because a repository URL or website link was sent. An authorised Teedee representative must demonstrate independent access to the company's source, hosting, domain and data platforms and complete the agreed routine CMS tasks using the supplied instructions.
