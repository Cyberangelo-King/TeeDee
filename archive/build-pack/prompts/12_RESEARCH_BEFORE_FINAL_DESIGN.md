# Prompt 12 — Research and Design Decisions

Before locking final page structure and copy, conduct a focused, evidence-based market/user/competitor review for TeeDee Edge Automations LTD.

Use the research brief in `specs/11_RESEARCH_AND_COMPETITOR_BENCHMARK.md`.

Research current public websites of Nigerian and international providers across integrated security, CCTV, solar/inverter, electrical, access control, networking and building automation. Classify each as direct, adjacent or aspirational. Cite source URLs and access dates.

Do not pretend desk research is primary user research. Clearly separate:
- observed competitor facts;
- public customer reviews/feedback;
- inferred user needs;
- actual interview findings (only if interviews truly occurred);
- recommendations.

Produce a concise but substantive report and benchmark matrix. Focus on what should change in TeeDee's website:
- page structure;
- service taxonomy;
- content and terminology;
- trust and proof;
- photography/video;
- interactions;
- enquiry journey;
- mobile UX;
- accessibility and performance.

Do not copy competitor content or imagery. Do not invent company differentiators. Every recommendation must include rationale, evidence and priority.
