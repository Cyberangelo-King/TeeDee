# Prompt 9 — Motion and Interaction Polish

Audit the whole site and implement a coherent motion system.

Review:
- header scroll state;
- hero text entrance;
- section reveals;
- image masks/reveals;
- service card hover/focus;
- button response;
- carousels;
- FAQ accordion;
- route transitions;
- Lottie diagrams;
- loading feedback;
- mobile touch behaviour.

Rules:
- every animation must have a reason;
- no scroll hijacking;
- no excessive parallax;
- no animation that hides content;
- no hover-only information;
- keyboard and touch equivalents;
- reduced-motion fallback;
- avoid animating layout-heavy properties;
- code-split heavy animation modules;
- remove redundant libraries/effects.

Capture before/after screenshots or short recordings. Test on a mid-range mobile device/emulation. Record any motion that is intentionally omitted for performance or accessibility.
