# Project Brief

## Project
TeeDee Edge Automations LTD — corporate website and digital experience.

## One-line purpose
Create a premium, trustworthy, highly visual website that makes TeeDee's security, power, electrical, connectivity and automation capabilities understandable, credible and easy to enquire about.

## Strategic proposition
**Secure. Power. Automate.**

TeeDee Edge is presented as a practical systems partner for safer, powered and better-connected spaces. The site must translate technical services into clear customer outcomes: what problem is solved, where the solution fits, what the process involves and how to start a conversation.

## Primary outcomes
1. Make the company look established, capable and technically competent without exaggerating its scale.
2. Help residential, commercial, institutional and property/facility buyers understand relevant services.
3. Generate qualified enquiries through clear calls to action and low-friction contact paths.
4. Show real evidence of workmanship and process as the company supplies approved media.
5. Establish a reusable digital foundation for future projects, case studies, content and service additions.

## Target audiences
### A. Residential property owners and landlords
Need: security, backup power, electrical reliability, access and convenience.
Questions: What do I need? Can someone assess my property? What affects cost? How do I contact the company?

### B. Commercial premises and operators
Examples to validate: offices, shops, warehouses, clinics, schools and hospitality sites.
Need: site-specific systems, predictable scope, competent installation, documentation and support.
Questions: Can the solution fit the premises? What is the process? Can systems work together? How do we request an assessment?

### C. Property and facility managers
Need: maintainable systems, upgrades, access/security, power continuity and clear accountability.
Questions: Can TeeDee assess an existing installation? What is included? What happens after installation?

### D. Institutions, contractors and project partners
Need: a credible capability profile, clear service boundaries, coordination and quotation discipline.
Questions: What can TeeDee deliver? What information is needed for a quote? How do we discuss a project?

These are strategic audience hypotheses, not verified customer research. Validate with management and actual enquiry history.

## Brand personality
- Capable: show method, equipment, scope and process.
- Precise: use specific service names and clean information architecture.
- Assured: calm, confident, honest language.
- Accessible: explain technical concepts without talking down to people.

## Creative direction
Use Ucam Home 3 as the primary visual reference, especially the video-background hero variation. Preserve its broad section architecture, visual rhythm, image-led storytelling, transitions and polished presentation. Do not copy its proprietary code, branding, written copy or unlicensed images. Rebuild the experience using original implementation and TeeDee assets.

The result should feel like a premium engineering and integrated-systems company, not a generic CCTV installer template.

## Success measures
Baseline first; do not invent targets. Track:
- qualified enquiries;
- enquiry source;
- click-throughs on primary enquiry CTAs;
- completed contact forms;
- WhatsApp/call/email actions;
- service-page engagement;
- case-study engagement;
- page speed and Core Web Vitals;
- accessibility and mobile usability.

## Scope boundaries
### In scope
- Marketing website;
- responsive layouts;
- brand implementation;
- content architecture and copy;
- media/asset direction;
- motion and micro-interactions;
- contact/enquiry journey;
- basic analytics readiness;
- SEO foundations;
- deployment-ready code and documentation.

### Out of scope for first release unless explicitly approved
- customer portal or account login;
- payment processing;
- online quotation/pricing engine;
- CRM implementation;
- booking calendar;
- AI chatbot or automated pricing commitments;
- admin CMS with authentication;
- claims of 24/7 support;
- complex backend or database.

## Key constraints
- Existing company logo and flyer already exist and must be used as source materials.
- No fabricated facts, claims, project history or testimonials.
- Performance matters: video and motion must not compromise usability.
- All important content must remain available without animation.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Contact details must come from the supplied flyer or an explicitly approved company source.
